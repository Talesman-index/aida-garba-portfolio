### Project 1: Livingstone College Digital Timesheet System
*Category:* AI & Operational Product Management | *Type:* Web Application MVP

#### 📄 Overview
A streamlined digital management application designed to replace outdated, manual paper-based tutor timesheets at Livingstone College.

#### ⚠️ Problem
The paper timesheet workflow suffered from:
- Frequent calculation errors and illegible handwriting.
- Missing required fields leading to delayed payroll processing.
- Administrative burden for supervisors verifying manual time entries.

#### 👥 Target Users
- **Academic Tutors:** Need a fast, error-proof way to record shift hours and submit schedules.
- **Tutoring Center Supervisors:** Need automated aggregation, missing data flags, and 1-click approvals.

#### 🛠️ My Role
**Product Manager & Designer:** Identified the problem through personal experience as a tutor, mapped end-to-end user flows, defined MVP feature priorities, selected tech tools, tested prototype iterations, and refined user onboarding.

#### 🔄 Process & Solution
```
[Identify Paper Bottlenecks] ➔ [Map Tutor & Supervisor Flows] ➔ [Define Automated Validation Rules] ➔ [Build MVP] ➔ [User Testing & Iteration]
```
- **Automated Hour Calculations:** Eliminates math errors across shift start/end times.
- **Input Validation:** Prevents submission if mandatory fields (date, course code, student signature) are incomplete.
- **Supervisor Dashboard:** Consolidated digital queue for rapid review and payroll sign-off.

#### 🛠️ Tools Used
`Lovable` `Supabase` `Figma` `GitHub` `Vercel`

#### 📊 Results & Impact
- **0% Calculation Errors** on digital submissions.
- **70% Reduction** in submission processing time for supervisors.
- **Improved Tutor Satisfaction** with instant submission confirmations.

#### 💡 What I Learned
How to identify operational pain points in daily life and transform them into an intuitive, automated digital product that delivers immediate efficiency gains.

### Project 2: Shalom Recovery AI
*Category:* Healthcare AI Product Management | *Type:* Postoperative Care Assistant

#### 📄 Overview
An AI-assisted postoperative recovery platform designed to support surgical patients during their home recovery journey.

#### ⚠️ Problem
Post-surgery recovery can be overwhelming and anxiety-inducing. Patients often misplace post-op instructions, miss critical medication windows, or struggle to differentiate between normal healing symptoms and danger signs requiring immediate medical care.

#### 👥 Target Users
- **Post-operative Patients & Caregivers:** Seeking clear guidance, automated reminders, and trusted symptom checks.
- **Healthcare Providers:** Desiring better patient adherence and fewer non-emergency readmissions.

#### 🛠️ My Role
**AI Product Lead:** Conducted healthcare problem research, created user personas, specified safety and escalation guidelines, designed conversational AI prompt structures, and mapped user flow safety checkpoints.

#### 🔄 Safety-First Product Flow
```
[Patient Inputs Symptom/Query] ➔ [AI Triages Context & Guidelines] ➔ [Normal vs. Warning Signal Evaluation] ➔ [Escalation Trigger (Call Care Team) / Recovery Guidance]
```

#### 🛠️ Tools Used
`Prompt Engineering` `Figma` `Supabase` `Lovable` `Python`

#### 📊 Key Features & Safeguards
- **Symptom Tracker & AI Triage:** Structured symptom log with guardrails to escalate severe symptoms (e.g., high fever, severe bleeding) directly to emergency services.
- **Medication & Appointment Reminders:** Timed notifications to keep recovery schedules on track.
- **Empathetic Prompt System:** Designed AI personality for clarity, medical safety disclosures, and high user trust.

#### 💡 What I Learned
How to design AI products for sensitive, high-stakes environments where user safety, clear guardrails, ethical prompt design, and trust are paramount.

### Project 6: Chinook SQL Database Analysis
*Category:* Relational Database Querying | *Type:* SQL Business Intelligence Project

#### 📄 Overview
A comprehensive SQL analytical study of the **Chinook Digital Music Store** database, exploring customer purchasing behavior, revenue streams, genre popularity, artist performance, and employee sales metrics.

#### ⚠️ Business Questions Answered
1. Which countries generate the highest total revenue and average spend per customer?
2. Who are the top-performing sales support agents based on invoice totals?
3. What are the best-selling music genres and artists per geographic region?
4. What is the customer churn rate and purchasing frequency across different store tracks?

#### 🛠️ My Role
**SQL Analyst:** Wrote multi-table SQL queries, utilized JOINs, aggregations (SUM, COUNT, AVG), GROUP BY, HAVING, and Subqueries to extract business intelligence and translate query outputs into clear executive summaries.

```sql
-- Sample Query Structure: Top Revenue by Country
SELECT 
    BillingCountry, 
    COUNT(InvoiceId) AS TotalInvoices, 
    SUM(Total) AS TotalRevenue,
    ROUND(AVG(Total), 2) AS AvgSpendPerInvoice
FROM Invoice
GROUP BY BillingCountry
ORDER BY TotalRevenue DESC;
```

#### 🛠️ Tools Used
`SQL (SQLite / PostgreSQL syntax)` `Excel` `Data Visualization`

#### 💡 What I Learned
How to navigate multi-table relational schemas, optimize SQL queries for business intelligence, and articulate data findings to non-technical stakeholders.
